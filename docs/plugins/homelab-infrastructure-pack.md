# Homelab & Infrastructure plugin pack

This pack models devices, networks, VLANs, VMs, containers, services, and domains as an independent infrastructure graph. Its operations log covers deployments, changes, incidents, maintenance, backups, restore tests, and experiments, including shared Planner/Calendar day blocks.

The dashboard surfaces degraded or offline assets, failed work, backup coverage for critical assets, and restore-test confidence. Research outputs, equipment ownership, and purchases remain owned by Research Lab, Home Inventory, and Wishlist.

Platform-specific Portainer, Docker, MikroTik, Prometheus, Grafana, or Kubernetes connections remain optional future adapters.
