# Personal Life plugin pack

The Personal Life pack groups independent specialist stores behind a dedicated
**Life** workspace mode. It complements PARA: projects and areas remain in the
canonical PARA store while personal operational records stay inside the plugin
that owns their workflow.

| Plugin | Contribution |
|---|---|
| Routines & Habits | Recurring Planner-block actions, targets, check-ins, and streaks |
| Meal Planner | Recipes, weekly meals, pantry-aware shopping, trips, and meal prep |
| Workout Planner | Sessions, exercises, prescriptions, completion, and Planner blocks |
| Health Tracker | Appointments, medication, symptoms, measurements, and care history |
| Home & Maintenance | Chores, repairs, appliances, warranties, manuals, and service history |
| Home Inventory | Possessions, storage, serials, receipts, lending, and insurance values |
| Finance & Subscriptions | Bills, subscriptions, budgets, savings goals, and payment history |
| Personal CRM | Relationships, birthdays, follow-ups, interactions, and gift ideas |
| Travel Planner | Trips, bookings, itinerary logs, packing, destinations, and documents |
| Hobby Studio | Projects, practice, skills, materials, equipment, and milestones |
| Wishlist & Purchases | Comparisons, prices, vendors, orders, returns, and warranties |
| Places Library | Saved places, planned visits, ratings, things to try, and visit history |
| Journal & Reflection | Mood, energy, gratitude, prompts, highlights, and lessons |
| Media Library | Written, watched, listened, played, learned, and live media |

The pack also includes Planner and Calendar. Plugins with a schedule expose a
date, recurrence, optional end date, and shared day-block field. Every plugin
persists to its own `modulo-life-<plugin-id>-v1` envelope; disabling a plugin
removes its view without deleting its data.
