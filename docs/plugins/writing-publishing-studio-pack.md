# Writing & Publishing Studio

This pack adds a writing pipeline around Modulo's existing Markdown Notes and Planner:

- **Manuscripts** covers books, essays, articles, stories, scripts, premises, audiences, word counts, and writing sessions.
- **Editorial Workflow** covers versions, structural editing, copy editing, fact checking, feedback, and approvals.
- **Submissions & Publishing** covers pitches, submissions, guidelines, rights, payments, releases, and published URLs.
- **Writing Dashboard** surfaces manuscript momentum, editorial queues, submissions, deadlines, and tracked word counts.

Markdown Notes remains the preferred owner of prose. The studio stores workflow metadata and links to drafts rather than creating another document format.
