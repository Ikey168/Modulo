# Research Evidence Lab

The Research Evidence Lab complements Information Intake Modes with durable evidence structure:

- **Sources & Bibliography** stores papers, books, web sources, datasets, quotations, citation keys, identifiers, and locators.
- **Claims & Evidence Map** records claims, supporting evidence, counterevidence, confidence, dependencies, and qualifying relationships.
- **Reproducibility Records** stores procedures, environments, datasets, versions, commits, runs, results, and artifacts.
- **Evidence Lab Dashboard** surfaces extraction work, contested claims, scheduled runs, and reproducibility status.

Source and claim IDs create provider-neutral relationships. A future citation-manager connector can synchronize metadata without becoming the owner of claims or research conclusions.
