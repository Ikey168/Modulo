# Career Studio

Career Studio separates durable career evidence from opportunity execution:

- **Career Portfolio** covers roles, CV variants, accomplishments, portfolio items, impact, and evidence links.
- **Applications & Interviews** covers target roles, applications, contacts, interview stages, offers, and follow-ups.
- **Professional Development** covers skills, certifications, mentorship, learning goals, and career experiments.
- **Career Dashboard** surfaces active opportunities, scheduled actions, portfolio readiness, and development progress.

The pack is local-first and does not submit applications, contact employers, scrape job sites, or expose records to external services.
