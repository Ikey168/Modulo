# Wardrobe & Style plugin pack

This pack separates the physical closet from creative style work:

- **Wardrobe** tracks garments, categories, fit, color, seasons, storage, care states, and optional references to Inventory or Wishlist records.
- **Style Studio** composes garments into outfits and capsules, records silhouettes, archetypes, occasions, wears, experiments, repairs, alterations, decluttering, and capsule reviews.
- **Style Dashboard** identifies garment-care work, items outside saved looks, capsule-review gaps, and completed experiments.

Scheduled experiments and care work appear in shared Planner and Calendar blocks.
