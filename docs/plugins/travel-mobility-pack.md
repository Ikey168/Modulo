# Travel & Mobility

Travel & Mobility extends the existing Travel Planner without duplicating trip records.

- **Travel Planner** remains the source for trips, bookings, itineraries, packing, and travel documents.
- **Vehicles** covers cars, bicycles, motorcycles, shared vehicles, ownership, specifications, condition, and value.
- **Mileage & Maintenance** covers mileage, inspections, service, repairs, fuel, charging, costs, and recurring maintenance.
- **Permits & Transport Passes** covers licenses, registrations, insurance, permits, toll products, and public-transport passes.
- **Travel & Mobility Dashboard** combines mobility due dates and provides a direct route to Travel Planner.

The pack does not connect to vehicle telemetry, insurers, mapping providers, or transport operators. Those can be added as optional sources later.
