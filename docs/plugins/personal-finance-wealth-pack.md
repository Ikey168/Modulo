# Personal Finance & Wealth

This provider-neutral pack adds:

- **Accounts, Assets & Liabilities** for balance-sheet records and valuation snapshots.
- **Cash Flow & Savings** for income, expenses, transfers, budgets, and recurring contributions.
- **Investments & Goals** for holdings, portfolios, allocation notes, savings goals, and retirement targets.
- **Wealth Dashboard** for tracked net value, due items, reviews, and goals.

Values are manual snapshots. The pack does not connect to banks or brokers, initiate transactions, store banking credentials, calculate tax, or provide financial advice. Those integrations can remain independent data providers later.
