# TTRPG Campaign Studio plugin pack

This pack supports Cyberpunk RED, Shadowrun, D&D, and other systems without imposing a rules engine.

- **Campaigns** owns table status, system, cadence, dates, location, GM, players, tone, and safety agreements.
- **World & Characters** stores PCs, NPCs, locations, factions, quests, items, lore, encounters, and handouts.
- **Sessions** connects scheduling and attendance to prep, recaps, decisions, consequences, and next hooks.
- **Campaign Dashboard** surfaces active tables, upcoming sessions, continuity gaps, world depth, and the real people at the table.

Sessions use shared Planner and Calendar day blocks. Notes and Personal CRM remain the owners of freeform documents and relationship records.
